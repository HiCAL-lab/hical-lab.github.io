## Papers linked but not copied for imagery

The site links AIAA and other publisher-hosted work where appropriate. Figures from those papers are not bundled into this repository unless reuse permission is clear. If author-owned original figure exports are available and may be posted publicly, place them in `assets/` and update the corresponding `image` entry in `site-data.js`.

## Recommended long-term practice

For a durable academic site, replace remote publisher image URLs with author-controlled figure exports in `assets/` while preserving a short citation beneath each figure. This avoids broken hotlinks and keeps figure provenance explicit.
